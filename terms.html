Personal use information

Home Assistant Nest is a personal connection for a household’s own authorized thermostat. These pages explain that use and do not offer a public thermostat service or create a separate customer contract.

Household access

The Google account holder chooses whether to authorize the connection. The household’s Home Assistant administrator manages who can access its dashboard and controls. Use is limited to devices and functions that the account holder has authorized.

Device operation and availability

Features depend on the thermostat’s supported functions, its settings, internet access, Google services, and Home Assistant. Readings or commands may be delayed or unavailable. Existing thermostat safety limits and physical controls continue to govern operation.

Keeping control of the connection

The account holder can revoke Google authorization and remove the saved integration in Home Assistant. The privacy information explains what the connection uses and how saved records are managed.

Google, Home Assistant, and any remote access or hosting providers have their own applicable terms. This page does not replace those terms.

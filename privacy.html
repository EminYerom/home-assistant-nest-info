Personal household integration

Privacy information

This connection uses authorized thermostat information to show its state and provide controls in a private household’s Home Assistant installation.

Information accessed

The connection uses the selected thermostat’s temperature, humidity, mode, target temperatures, supported fan state, and device information needed to identify it. Only the thermostat selected during Google authorization is used for this connection.

Google authorization also includes permission to view and manage Google Cloud Pub/Sub topics and subscriptions. After setup and authorization are complete, Home Assistant is intended to use a dedicated topic and subscription to receive thermostat updates.

How information is used and shared

Google Nest and Google Cloud Pub/Sub process device information and events and deliver updates to Home Assistant. Authorized Home Assistant users can view the thermostat and use its supported controls, including through the household’s configured remote access.

This household connection does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or model training. The Nest thermostat is not exposed to Gemini. Google operates its services under its own privacy policy.

Storage and access

Home Assistant stores the connection’s authorization tokens and configuration. It may record thermostat states in history and include connection information in logs or backups, depending on the installation’s settings. Its administrator controls user access and manages those settings.

Retention depends on the settings and backups in use. No fixed retention period or automatic deletion schedule is promised here.

Revoking the connection

The account holder can revoke the connection in Google Account permissions or Nest Partner Connections Manager and remove the saved Nest integration in Home Assistant. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access; it does not by itself erase previously saved history, logs, or backups. The household’s Home Assistant administrator manages those records.

These information pages

These static pages contain no thermostat readings, credentials, analytics, tracking scripts, forms, or account sign-in. A hosting provider may process ordinary web request information under its own policies.

The connection’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Questions about this personal connection can be directed to the household’s Home Assistant administrator or the support contact shown on Google’s consent screen.

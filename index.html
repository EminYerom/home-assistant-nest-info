Personal household integration

Thermostat control at home and away.

Home Assistant Nest connects an authorized household thermostat to a private Home Assistant installation, so household members can check the temperature and use its controls in one place.

Setup is in progress. These pages describe the intended connection; final authorization and checks are still pending. Once connected, thermostat readings and controls will be available through the household’s Home Assistant app to its authorized users.

What the connection does

  • Shows the authorized thermostat’s temperature, humidity, operating mode, and target temperatures.
  • Provides supported temperature, mode, and fan controls through Home Assistant.
  • Receives device updates through Google Nest and Google Cloud Pub/Sub.

Permission stays with the account holder

The Google account holder selects the thermostat during authorization and can revoke the connection later. Access to the household dashboard depends on Home Assistant account permissions.

The thermostat is not exposed to the household’s Gemini assistant. Read the privacy information for details about permissions, storage, and revoking access.
